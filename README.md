## 💡 Executive Summary & The Problem

Traditional prediction markets suffer from order-book liquidity starvation on niche or long-tail topics. When thin order books dry up, spreads blow out, and retail participants cannot enter without suffering extreme slippage.

**Daejin** re-architects prediction markets on Solana around a **creator-owned shared-pool parimutuel primitive**:
1. **Zero Cold-Start Friction:** Anyone can launch a market for free with a verifiable outcome question and resolution proof source.
2. **Shared-Pool Price Discovery:** Outcome probability is determined purely by the crowd's capital conviction ($\text{Price} = \frac{\text{Pool}_{\text{Side}}}{\text{Pool}_{\text{Total}}}$).
3. **Creator Economy Monetization:** 50% of protocol fees generated by trading volume route directly to the market creator's wallet.
4. **On-Chain Verifiable Resolution:** Markets settle deterministically against published, timestamped evidence sources.

The **Daejin Market Creator Toolkit** provides an open-source mathematical simulation engine, interactive terminal, and complete educational walkthrough for creators launching on Solana.

## 🧮 Shared-Pool Parimutuel Mathematical Engine

Let:
- $Y_0, N_0$: Initial liquidity in the YES and NO pools ($ USD)
- $S$: User investment stake ($ USD, $\ge \$5$)
- $f$: Market fee rate in basis points (e.g., $200\text{ bps} = 2\%$)
- $S_{net} = S \times (1 - f)$: Net capital entering the pool
- $f_{creator} = \frac{S \times f}{2}$: Creator fee revenue allocation
- $f_{protocol} = \frac{S \times f}{2}$: Protocol fee allocation

When a user backs the YES side:
$$Y_1 = Y_0 + S_{net}, \quad N_1 = N_0$$
$$T = Y_1 + N_1$$

The real-time market-implied probabilities become:
$$P(\text{YES}) = \frac{Y_1}{T}, \quad P(\text{NO}) = \frac{N_1}{T}$$

If the YES outcome resolves true, winning stakeholders split the entire opposing pool in exact proportion to their net stake:
$$\text{Payout}_{\text{YES}} = \left(\frac{S_{net}}{Y_1}\right) \times T$$
$$\text{ROI} = \left(\frac{\text{Payout}_{\text{YES}} - S}{S}\right) \times 100\%$$

For a market with $Y_0 = \$1,000$, $N_0 = \$2,000$, and a $\$50$ YES position:
- $S_{net} = \$49.00$
- $Y_1 = \$1,049.00$, $N_1 = \$2,000.00$, $T = \$3,049.00$
- $P(\text{YES}) = 34.4\%$
- $\text{Payout} = \frac{49}{1049} \times 3049 = \$142.47$
- $\text{ROI} = +184.9\%$

## 🧵 Educational Content & Architecture Breakdown for X

Below is the thread content created for the Daejin launch challenge:

1. **What is Daejin?**  
   Daejin turns anyone into a prediction market creator on Solana. No market maker agreements or order-book matching needed. You ask a question, define the proof source, and traders price the truth in real time with shared pools. 🎯🔮

2. **How do Shared Pools work?**  
   Unlike order books that require a matching counterparty at your exact limit price, Daejin pools all bets on YES and NO. As money flows in, the price is simply `PoolSide / TotalPool`. When the market resolves, winners split the whole opposing pool!

3. **Creator Monetization:**  
   Creators earn 50% of all trading fees generated by their markets. If your market drives $100k in volume, you pocket sustainable protocol yield straight to your Solana wallet.

4. **Verifiable Proof:**  
   Every market binds to a public, immutable source (CoinGecko, official sports feeds, government statistics). Resolvers sign the outcome on-chain with zero guesswork.
